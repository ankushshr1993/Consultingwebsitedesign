# Design: Regression / Ink

## System
- Hallmark Ink, deal 1/3, seed investor-executive-hires-operator-4a32·ec4e9ac7·2026-11-03·r0.
- Full redesign, editorial. Light paper, Epilogue 700 display / 400 body, flat ruled compositions.
- Preserve Regression's actual navy/gold logo and corporate accents; Ink's teal is replaced by the existing gold identity, with navy links for contrast.
- Shared source: apps/consulting/app/tokens.css and apps/defence-fund/app/tokens.css, generated by Hallmark's scaffold.
- Document-centered advisory dossiers: actual service outputs carry the visual identity; no stock photographs, invented biographies, metrics, or portfolio projects.
- Hero: Poster, from draw 5cccda; on supporting pages Banner and Long Document.
- The draw's portfolio and floating pill are replaced by a documented service index and a branded masthead, because no project pictures exist and the navigation must accommodate the actual routes.

## Tokens and controls
- Paper white, secondary paper pale gray; ink navy, subdued text blue-gray; gold reserved for brand marks and dark-band links.
- A single 1280–1440px capped container, 64px desktop gutter, 24px tablet, 16px phone.
- Shared Hallmark spacing scale and type roles. Epilogue is locally hosted via @fontsource/epilogue.
- Primary controls: navy fill, paper text, 10px radius, minimum 48px. Secondary: ruled outline.
- No resting shadows, gradients, cards within cards, or decorative animation. Focus rings and accessible errors remain explicit.

## Page composition
- Consulting home: poster thesis; service index; operating contexts as disclosures; method as a numbered notice; direct contact colophon.
- Services: numbered service chapters, with buyer and output in the margin and actions in the main column; engagement models as a comparison notice.
- Approach: vertical method timeline with explicit outputs.
- About: named contact and principles as an editorial letter, experience as a ruled index.
- Defence home: poster thesis; founder/investor decision rows; operating examples; readiness method; direct contact.
- Defence criteria/thesis/process: long documents with a side index and ruled sections, never grids of identical bordered cards.
- Resources, FAQ, forms, and policies share the same typography, surface and controls; form logic is preserved.

## Preservation
Keep every route, existing content and claim, destination, form name/id and handler, event, validation and provider acceptance check. Terms, consent and policy wording stays verbatim. No files or component trees are deleted. Tighten marketing copy only where Hallmark identifies repetition; record changes.
