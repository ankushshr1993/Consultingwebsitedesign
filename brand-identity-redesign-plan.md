# Regression Consulting — Brand Identity & Positioning Refresh Plan

## What to preserve

- **Core positioning:** “When technology execution becomes enterprise risk.”
- **Selective buyer signal:** Keep and expand the “Not a Fit” section.
- **Direct tone:** Maintain concise, operator-level language.

## Priority problems to solve

1. No visible human leadership presence.
2. No explained brand story behind “Regression Consulting.”
3. No distinct visual identity system.
4. Flat typography hierarchy and weak numeric/data expression.

## Homepage content additions (high impact, low scope)

Add a short section immediately after the hero:

- Headline: **“Operator-led, with enterprise scar tissue.”**
- 80–120 words with:
  - Name
  - One-sentence background
  - 2–3 outcomes delivered (without violating confidentiality)
  - Link to full bio/credentials

Add one paragraph near the first methodology section:

> Regression is the discipline of finding true signal through noisy data. We use the same principle in technology diligence and execution recovery: isolate what is causal, remove what is cosmetic, and return systems to a stable operating baseline.

Introduce a “Selected Outcomes” strip:

- “Stabilized post-acquisition platform in 90 days”

Format as compact metric cards with muted context notes.

## Visual identity direction

### Color system

- **Base:** `#0B1020` (deep navy)
- **Surface:** `#111831`
- **Text primary:** `#E8ECF8`
- **Text secondary:** `#A8B3CF`
- **Accent:** `#4DA3FF` (electric blue) or `#F5B544` (amber)
- **Dividers/grid:** `#2A3559`

### Graphic language

Use a recurring “signal vs noise” motif:

- Background scatter points at low opacity
- Single linear trend line as accent
- Light grid overlays for section transitions
- Minimal animation on trend-line draw during hero load

### Motion

- 180–240ms hover transitions
- 300–500ms section reveal on first viewport entry
- Subtle parallax or transform on hero graphic (max 6–10px)

Keep motion restrained and utilitarian.

## Typography system

### Recommended stack

- **Display/hero:** `Syne` (800) or `Space Grotesk` (700)
- **Body:** `DM Sans` (400/500)
- **Data labels + nav:** `IBM Plex Mono` (500)

### Hierarchy rules

- H1: 56–72px, tight leading, slight negative tracking
- H2: 32–40px, medium tracking
- Eyebrow/labels: 11–13px mono uppercase, +10–14% tracking
- Body max line length: 65–75ch
- Step numbers (`01/02/03`): mono, oversized, accent-colored, low-opacity shadow layer
